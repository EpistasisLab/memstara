# Bottom line

Long-term joint exposure to multiple air pollutants was associated with higher incident heart failure risk in a dose-response, additive manner. The association was strengthened by genetic susceptibility to HF.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort analysis in UK Biobank, median follow-up 10.1 years
- **Population** — UK Biobank adults free of HF, atrial fibrillation or coronary heart disease at baseline (2006-2010)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during follow-up to 2018 (4201 cases); ascertainment method not given in abstract
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.59) — nothing to score on size or adjustment
