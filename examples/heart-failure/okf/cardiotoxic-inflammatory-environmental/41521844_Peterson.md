# Bottom line

An immune-enriched proteomic cluster higher in people with HIV was associated with myocardial fibrosis regardless of serostatus and predicted incident HF in a community cohort without HIV (aHR 1.71 per SD). 34 of 39 individual proteins also predicted HF.

- **Design** — Discovery cross-sectional proteomic analysis (SMASH) with external validation in MESA cross-sectional (ECV) and prospective cohort (incident HF)
- **N** — 3,223
- **Population** — SMASH: 342 people with/without HIV with CMR; MESA: 3223 community adults without HIV or prevalent HF (522 with CMR)
- **Outcome** — Elevated CMR extracellular volume fraction (ECV ≥30% women, ≥28% men); incident adjudicated heart failure in MESA (HFpEF LVEF ≥50%, HFrEF <40%)
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.81)

3. The strongest of these was IGFBP4 (adjusted HR [aHR], 1.89 per SD [95% CI, 1.51‒2.38]; FDR = 8.43 × 10−7).
4. Both proteins associated with high ECV in MESA were also associated with time to incident HF: PIGR (FDR = 1.04 × 10−4) and FOLR2 (FDR = 0.028), with similar point estimates by HF subtype for PIGR, and a higher point estimate comparing HFpEF to HFrEF for FOLR2 (Table 2).

6. Here, we found that higher plasma level of this cluster was also associated with elevated ECV (adjusted PR [aPR], 1.13 per SD increment in plasma cluster [95% confidence interval {CI}, 1.02‒1.25]; P = .02, Figure 3A).
7. The overall prevalence of elevated ECV was 52% and was higher among PWH compared to PWOH (57% vs 45%, respectively; P = .05 for difference, adjusting for demographics, substance use, and traditional risk factors).
8. We tested each feature of the proteomic signature we identified in SMASH for associations with elevated ECV in MESA.

# Caveats

Only 118 HF events in MESA and few HF subtype cases; MESA validation population older and without HIV, and only 2 of 39 proteins replicated for ECV. Observational proteomic associations may reflect age and other unmeasured confounding; HIV-related inference is from discovery cohort only.
