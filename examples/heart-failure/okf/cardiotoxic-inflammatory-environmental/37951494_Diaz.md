# Bottom line

Female sex did not predict incident heart failure after anthracycline treatment in adults (HR 0.87; 0.94 in the competing-risk model). Age, coronary artery disease and hematopoietic stem cell transplant (and BMI in the competing-risk model) were associated with HF.

- **Design** — Single-center retrospective cohort study, Cox proportional hazards and competing risk models
- **N** — 1,525
- **Population** — Adults without prior HF or cardiomyopathy treated with anthracyclines 1992-2019 at a single center
- **Outcome** — New heart failure within 5 years of the first anthracycline dose
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.63)

# Caveats

Short median follow-up (about 1 year) and few events (73) limit power, so wide CIs do not exclude a sex effect. Single-center, retrospective; covariates and effect sizes for other predictors are not given in the abstract.
