# Bottom line

Osimertinib was associated with a roughly 2.3-2.6-fold higher subdistribution hazard of HF hospitalization than other lung cancer therapies, and 3.19-fold versus other EGFR TKIs. Prior HF, AF, age ≥65, hypertension and CKD were additional risk factors.

- **Design** — Retrospective cohort study using the Medical Data Vision claims database, Fine-Gray models with propensity score matching
- **Population** — Adults with lung cancer (ICD-10 C34) in Japan treated with osimertinib, other EGFR TKIs, ALK inhibitors or cytotoxic chemotherapy, 2008-2021
- **Outcome** — HF hospitalization: ICD-10 I50.0/I50.1/I50.9/I11.0 plus IV furosemide, vasodilators or inotropes within 2 days, occurring from 7 days after the index date to 30 days after the last prescription

# Caveats

Claims-based HF definition and comorbidities may be misclassified; no LVEF or cardiac biomarker data, and unmeasured confounding (e.g. smoking, prior anthracyclines) is possible. Follow-up was short (median 173 days), osimertinib patients were more often female and second-line, and control group was heterogeneous.
