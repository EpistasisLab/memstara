# Bottom line

NSAID use was associated with a modestly higher risk of incident heart failure (pooled RR 1.17), driven by conventional NSAIDs (RR 1.35) rather than COXIBs (RR 1.03).

- **Design** — Systematic review and meta-analysis of observational studies, random-effects generic inverse variance
- **N** — 7,543,805
- **Population** — Participants in 7 observational studies comparing NSAID users with nonusers
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure as reported in the included studies (OR, RR, HR or SIR)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.79) — nothing to score on adjustment
