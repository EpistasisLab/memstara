# Bottom line

In CVD-free adults, long-term aspirin use was associated with higher incident heart failure risk under g-formula hypothetical interventions. Never using aspirin lowered risk by 6% versus the natural course.

- **Design** — Patient-level pooled analysis of four prospective cohorts using the parametric g-formula with time-dependent exposure
- **N** — 26,941
- **Population** — Adults free of cardiovascular disease but at risk for heart failure, mean age 60.7, from four US prospective cohorts
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during follow-up (median 14.8 years); 5,899 cases
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.77)

# Caveats

Observational data with hypothetical g-formula interventions; residual confounding by indication (aspirin for risk) is likely. Aspirin dose and indication are not described in the abstract. Absolute effects are small.
