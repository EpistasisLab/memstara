# Bottom line

Short-term NSAID use was associated with increased odds of first-time HF hospitalization in patients with T2DM (OR 1.43; 95% CI 1.27-1.63). Risk was higher in those aged ≥80, with elevated HbA1c on 0-1 antidiabetic drugs, and in new NSAID users.

- **Design** — Nationwide Danish register-based case-crossover study with 28-day exposure windows
- **N** — 331,189
- **Population** — Danish adults newly diagnosed with T2DM 1998-2021, without prior HF, rheumatic disease, or NSAID use in preceding 120 days
- **Outcome** — First-time heart failure hospitalization during follow-up
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.61) — nothing to score on adjustment
