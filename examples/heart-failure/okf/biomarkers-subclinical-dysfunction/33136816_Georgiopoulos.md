# Bottom line

Imaging-detected LV systolic dysfunction, LV dilation and diastolic dysfunction independently predict incident heart failure in asymptomatic people. LV hypertrophy showed only a nonsignificant trend.

- **Design** — Systematic review and meta-analysis of 32 studies from 11 cohorts (TTE, CMR, CCT)
- **Population** — Asymptomatic individuals without heart failure in 11 cohorts with baseline cardiac imaging
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during follow-up in included cohort studies
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.79) — nothing to score on size
