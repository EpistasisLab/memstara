# Bottom line

44 kidney-function-related plasma proteins were associated with incident HF in fully adjusted models, 29 validated in CRIC, mainly for HFpEF rather than HFrEF. Mendelian randomization suggested Golgi membrane protein 1 is causally linked to HF and eGFR.

- **Design** — Prospective longitudinal cohort (ARIC) with aptamer proteomics, external validation in CRIC, and two-sample Mendelian randomization
- **Population** — ARIC study participants (community-based adults), validated in CRIC (chronic kidney disease cohort)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure (HF), also subtyped as HFpEF vs HFrEF; abstract gives no further definition
