# Bottom line

Each 10 ms increase in DESL carried HR 1.58 for incident HF, and each 1% increase in DESLsystole HR 1.74; associations stayed significant after adjustment. DESLsystole outperformed other systolic echo parameters for HF prediction.

- **Design** — Prospective population-based cohort with speckle tracking echocardiography, median 16-year follow-up
- **N** — 1,210
- **Population** — Low-risk general population adults (41% men, mean age 56 years)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure (90 events), also MI and cardiovascular death as separate endpoints
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.63)
