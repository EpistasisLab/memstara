# Bottom line

Meta-analysis of two older-adult cohorts found 119 proteins associated with incident HF (15 HFpEF, 11 HFrEF) at Bonferroni significance, and 55 of 66 novel associations replicated in ARIC. Mendelian randomization suggested 7 possibly causal proteins, but colocalization evidence was absent.

- **Design** — Plasma proteomic screen in two population-based cohorts (CHS, AGES-RS) with fixed-effects meta-analysis, external replication in ARIC, and two-sample Mendelian randomization with colocalization
- **N** — 8,599
- **Population** — Older adults in CHS and AGES-RS population-based cohorts; replication in ARIC
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure, and subtypes HFpEF and HFrEF; 1590 HF events (536 HFpEF, 471 HFrEF). Detailed adjudication not given in abstract.
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.74)
