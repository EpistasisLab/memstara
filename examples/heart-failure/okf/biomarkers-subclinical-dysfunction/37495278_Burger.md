# Bottom line

CRP is an independent risk marker of incident HF hospitalization in patients with established CVD (HR 1.10 per 1 mg/L; 2.22 top vs bottom quartile), for both HFrEF and HFpEF. The association persisted beyond 15 years and after adjusting for medication and interim MI.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort (UCC-SMART) with Cox proportional hazards models
- **N** — 8,089
- **Population** — Patients with established cardiovascular disease without prevalent heart failure, UCC-SMART cohort
- **Outcome** — Incident HF defined as first hospitalization for heart failure
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.74)

# Caveats

Abstract only; outcome is HF hospitalization, so outpatient HF may be missed. Population limited to established CVD, limiting generalizability to primary prevention. Observational design cannot establish causality; CRP measured once at baseline.
