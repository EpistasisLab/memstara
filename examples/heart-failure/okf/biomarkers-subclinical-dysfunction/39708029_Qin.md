# Bottom line

Hypertension and obesity had the highest population-attributable risk for new-onset HF, with larger relative risks in women. Inflammatory proteomic pathways (neutrophil degranulation) mediated 22-24% of hypertension and 33-47% of obesity associations with HF.

- **Design** — Prospective population-based cohort (UK Biobank) with proteomic pathway and mediation analyses
- **N** — 478,479
- **Population** — UK Biobank adults, men and women; proteomics subset 22,695 men and 27,421 women
- **Outcome** — New-onset (incident) heart failure during median 12 years follow-up
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.71) — nothing to score on adjustment
