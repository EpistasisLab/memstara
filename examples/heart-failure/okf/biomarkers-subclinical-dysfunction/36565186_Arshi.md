# Bottom line

Each 1-SD larger epicardial fat volume was associated with incident HF (HR 1.34, 95% CI 1.07-1.68) independent of CHD and AF, and with larger LV dimensions, lower LVEF and larger LA diameter. Associations with diastolic indices (E/A ratio, deceleration time) were null after adjustment.

- **Design** — Prospective population-based cohort (Rotterdam Study) with linear mixed models and Cox regression
- **N** — 2,103
- **Population** — Community-dwelling older adults (mean age 68, 54% women) free of HF, AF and CHD at baseline, Rotterdam
- **Outcome** — Incident HF from medical records, ascertained by a clinician, follow-up to 1 Jan 2016; also repeated echocardiographic LV dimensions, LVEF, LA diameter, E/A ratio, deceleration time
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.80)

# Caveats

Only 124 HF events, and HF subtypes (HFpEF/HFrEF) were not analysed. Sex-stratified associations lost significance in women after CHD/AF adjustment. Observational, with a mostly older, healthy-enough cohort that had CT; the mediation by LV mass is only suggested.
