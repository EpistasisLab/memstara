# Bottom line

Nine circulating proteins (GDF-15, TIM-1, TRAIL-R2, SPON1, MMP-12, follistatin, U-PAR, OPG, ST2) were associated with incident heart failure in pooled elderly cohorts after adjusting for established risk factors. Most were also tied to worse baseline LV systolic function, and only TIM-1 to worse diastolic function.

- **Design** — Two community-based prospective cohorts (PIVUS discovery, ULSAM replication) with proximity extension assay proteomics
- **Population** — Elderly community-dwelling individuals without heart failure at baseline (PIVUS age ~70, ULSAM men age ~78)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure (80 events in PIVUS, 90 in ULSAM); definition not given in abstract
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.71) — nothing to score on size
