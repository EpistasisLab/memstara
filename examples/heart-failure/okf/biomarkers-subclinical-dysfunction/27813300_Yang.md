# Bottom line

LA enlargement, LVH, impaired GLS and abnormal E/e' independently predicted incident HF; GLS and LV mass added incremental value over clinical parameters, with GLS significantly improving reclassification.

- **Design** — Community-based prospective cohort of asymptomatic elderly with HF risk factors, echo screening and ~14-month follow-up
- **N** — 410
- **Population** — Asymptomatic community adults ≥65 years with hypertension, type 2 diabetes or obesity; excluding valve disease, reduced EF and AF
- **Outcome** — New HF symptoms or cardiovascular death adjudicated by three independent cardiologists using Framingham criteria
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.61)
