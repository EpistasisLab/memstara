# Bottom line

The ARIC HF equation had modest discrimination in CKD (C 0.68) and was poorly calibrated; NT-proBNP and hsTnT together discriminated better, and a combined clinical+biomarker+echo model reached 0.765. Discrimination was lower for HFpEF than HFrEF.

- **Design** — Prospective multicenter cohort (CRIC) prediction analysis with Fine-Gray models and 10-fold cross-validated C-indices
- **N** — 2,147
- **Population** — Adults with mild-moderate CKD (eGFR 20-70) without prior heart failure in the CRIC study
- **Outcome** — Adjudicated hospitalization for HF (probable/definite by modified Framingham criteria) over 10 years from year 1 exam; HFpEF (LVEF>=50%) vs HFrEF (<50%) secondary
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.69)

# Caveats

Complete-case analysis excluded 862 for missing covariates, and 26% of HF events could not be classified by EF. The cohort is CKD-only with HF hospitalization as the outcome, and discrimination was poorer at lower eGFR. Model comparison is mostly by C-index with wide overlapping CIs.
