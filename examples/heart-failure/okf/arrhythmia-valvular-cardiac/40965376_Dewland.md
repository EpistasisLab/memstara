# Bottom line

Baseline ECG PVCs were associated with higher adjusted incident heart failure risk (HR 1.43), highest for epicardial and left ventricular origin. Outflow tract and right ventricular PVCs did not show a clear excess risk.

- **Design** — Pooled analysis of two population-based prospective cohorts (CHS and ARIC) with PVC location assessed from baseline 12-lead ECG
- **N** — 20,590
- **Population** — Ambulatory adults without prevalent heart failure from CHS and ARIC
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure over mean 19.2 years follow-up (definition not given in abstract)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.77)

# Caveats

Abstract only: covariates and HF definition not stated. Only 427 participants (2.1%) had PVCs, so location subgroups are small and the epicardial estimate is imprecise (CI 1.12-7.95). A single baseline 12-lead ECG captures few PVCs and location was judged by reviewers.
