# Bottom line

Incident HF occurred in 3.6% of community AF patients over 2 years, mostly with preserved EF. Traditional risk factors, permanent AF versus paroxysmal, and higher baseline heart rate independently predicted it.

- **Design** — Prospective registry cohort (ORBIT-AF) with multivariable Cox frailty regression models
- **N** — 6,545
- **Population** — Community-based adults with atrial fibrillation without heart failure at baseline, 173 US sites
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during 2-year follow-up; EF preserved (>40%) or reduced (<=40%)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.74)
