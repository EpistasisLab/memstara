# Bottom line

Multiform PVCs were independently associated with new-onset heart failure (HR 1.456, 95% CI 1.062-1.997) compared with no PVC, in people with apparently normal hearts. Multiform PVCs also predicted death, hospitalization, TIA and AF.

- **Design** — Retrospective analysis of a 24-hour ECG monitoring database with long-term follow-up; Kaplan-Meier and multivariate Cox models
- **N** — 3,351
- **Population** — Adults with apparently structurally normal hearts undergoing 24-hour ECG monitoring 2002-2004
- **Outcome** — Incident new-onset heart failure during average 10±1 years follow-up (detailed ascertainment not given in abstract)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.65)
