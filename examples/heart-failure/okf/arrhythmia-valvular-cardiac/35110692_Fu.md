# Bottom line

After a myocarditis hospitalization, 6.4% developed incident HF/DCM within 1 year and 9.3% over follow-up, far more than controls and especially in those aged ≥50. HF/DCM incidence declined over calendar time.

- **Design** — Population-based register cohort (National Patient and Cause of Death Registers) with matched reference population and hospital record validation substudy
- **N** — 8,679
- **Population** — Adults ≥16 years in Sweden with first hospitalization for myocarditis 2000–2014 and age/sex-matched controls
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure/dilated cardiomyopathy hospital diagnosis in the National Patient Register after index myocarditis hospitalization, within 1 year and over whole study period
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.55)

# Caveats

Administrative diagnoses with only 83% validation at one centre; no exact diagnostic criteria. HF/DCM predictors were not modelled; the HF outcome is descriptive, unadjusted percentages, and prior comorbidities are baseline comparisons. Controls' HF/DCM follow-up rates are not directly comparable by HR.
