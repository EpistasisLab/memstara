# Bottom line

About 1 in 9 AF-hospitalised patients had incident HF within 3 years, and comorbidities and lifestyle factors independently predicted it. New HF carried an adjusted HR of 3.3 for all-cause death.

- **Design** — Retrospective population-based linked administrative data cohort (Western Australian Hospitalisation Morbidity Data Collection), 2000-2013
- **Population** — Adults aged 25-94 with first-in-period AF hospitalisation and no prior HF admission, Western Australia
- **Outcome** — First HF hospitalisation within 3 years of AF discharge
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.58) — nothing to score on size
