# Bottom line

Higher self-reported sedentary time and sitting time were dose-dependently associated with incident HF hospitalization in postmenopausal women, independent of recreational physical activity and comorbidities. Sedentary time >9.5 h/d carried a PAR of 7.3%.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort analysis within the Women's Health Initiative Observational Study with time-varying exposures and Cox regression
- **N** — 80,982
- **Population** — Postmenopausal US women aged 50-79 without baseline HF, able to walk one block, WHI Observational Study
- **Outcome** — Physician-adjudicated acute HF hospitalization from medical records (1,402 cases over ~9 years mean follow-up)
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.89)

# Caveats

Self-reported sedentary time with modest reliability (kappa 0.60) and weak correlation with accelerometry (r=0.31). Observational, mostly white older women; residual confounding and reverse causation possible. HF hospitalization only, not all HF.
