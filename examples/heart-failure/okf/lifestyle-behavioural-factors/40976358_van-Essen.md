# Bottom line

Six multimorbidity clusters differed in incident HF risk; the obese/physical inactivity cluster had aHR 3.80 versus the young cluster, with elderly (2.46) and pulmonary (2.10) clusters also elevated. The obesity/inactivity cluster skewed toward HFpEF.

- **Design** — Prospective observational population-based cohort (PREVEND) with latent class analysis of multimorbidity
- **N** — 6,839
- **Population** — Adults in the Dutch PREVEND community cohort, mean age 53.8 years, 50% women
- **Outcome** — New-onset heart failure, subdivided into HFrEF (390) and HFpEF (220) during 110,621 person-years of follow-up
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.74)
