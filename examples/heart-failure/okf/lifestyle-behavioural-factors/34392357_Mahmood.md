# Bottom line

Insomnia symptoms, cumulatively and individually, were associated with higher hazard of incident heart failure in a US older-adult cohort. Risk rose in a graded way from one to four symptoms.

- **Design** — Population-based prospective cohort (Health and Retirement Study) with marginal structural discrete-time survival analysis
- **N** — 12,761
- **Population** — US adults aged 50+ free of heart failure at baseline in 2002, mean age 66.7, 57.7% female
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure over 16 years of follow-up (1,730 cases); definition not detailed in abstract
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.75)
