# Bottom line

Women reporting both snoring and excessive daytime sleepiness (OSA symptoms) had about twice the adjusted risk of incident heart failure over follow-up to 2011. 5.3% vs 0.9% developed HF in the exposed and reference groups.

- **Design** — Population-based prospective cohort (Sleep and Health in Women, SHE) with register-linked follow-up
- **N** — 5,990
- **Population** — Swedish women from a population-based cohort, baseline questionnaire April 2000
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure from the Swedish National Patient Register and Cause of Death Register until 31 December 2011
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.73)
