# Bottom line

Genetically predicted obstructive sleep apnea was causally associated with heart failure (IVW OR 1.26), and the association persisted after multivariable adjustment (OR 1.13). Apolipoprotein B mediated about 9% of the effect.

- **Design** — Two-sample Mendelian randomization (univariable, multivariable, mediation) using FinnGen, UK Biobank and genetic consortia data
- **Population** — General population, largely European ancestry (FinnGen OSA GWAS)
- **Outcome** — Heart failure from UK Biobank and genetic consortia GWAS summary statistics; the abstract gives no further definition.
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — limited (0.43) — nothing to score on size
