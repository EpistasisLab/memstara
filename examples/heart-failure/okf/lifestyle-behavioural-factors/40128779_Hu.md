# Bottom line

Moderate and high LE8 cardiovascular health were associated with 40% and 54% lower HF risk versus low CVH, and each SD higher LE8 gave HR 0.745. Depressive symptoms increased HF risk (HR 1.309) and showed multiplicative and additive interaction with CVH.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort study (UK Biobank), Cox proportional hazards models
- **N** — 336,939
- **Population** — UK Biobank adults aged 37-73 without baseline HF, CHD or stroke
- **Outcome** — Incident HF by ICD-10 I50 from primary care, hospital admissions, self-report and death registries; median follow-up 13.6 years
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.93)

# Caveats

Observational UK Biobank cohort with healthy-volunteer selection; large exclusion for missing LE8 data (n=134,499). LE8 measured once at baseline with a modified diet score and sleep duration only; depressive symptoms partly self-reported. Residual confounding possible, and LE8 includes biological factors, so lifestyle effects are not isolated.
