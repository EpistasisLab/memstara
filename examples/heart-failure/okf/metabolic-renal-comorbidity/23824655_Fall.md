# Bottom line

Using FTO as an instrument, each BMI unit increase causally raised incident heart failure risk (HR 1.19, 95% CI 1.03-1.39), in line with the observational estimate. No causal effect was found for CHD or stroke.

- **Design** — Mendelian randomization meta-analysis of 36 cross-sectional and longitudinal population cohorts (ENGAGE consortium)
- **N** — 198,502
- **Population** — Adults of European descent from 36 population-based studies
- **Outcome** — Ever and incident heart failure (HR for incident, OR for ever) among nine dichotomous cardiometabolic outcomes; heart failure case definition not given in the text provided.
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.81) — nothing to score on design

# Caveats

MR assumes FTO affects outcomes only via BMI; pleiotropy not excluded, and a single variant was used. Heart failure estimates are borderline (CI lower bound 1.03) with 2,863 incident cases. Only European-descent individuals were studied; BMI measured once may understate lifetime adiposity.
