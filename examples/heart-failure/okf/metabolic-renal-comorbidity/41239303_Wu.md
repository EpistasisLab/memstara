# Bottom line

Long-term high or rising CVAI was associated with higher incident HF risk independent of conventional risk factors and baseline CVAI. The association was stronger in those under 60 and without hypertension.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort study (Kailuan) with latent mixture trajectory modelling and Cox regression
- **N** — 76,822
- **Population** — Active and retired employees of the Kailuan Group, China, free of HF and cancer in 2012
- **Outcome** — First HF event 2012-2020 from hospital discharge records, ICD-10 I50.x, confirmed with 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA criteria (NYHA/Killip class >=II, LVEF <=50%, high NT-proBNP)
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.89)

# Caveats

Men were 78% of the cohort and occupational Chinese cohort limits generalisability. Low-stable group had only 13 events, giving wide CIs, and CVAI includes age, BMI, WC, TG and HDL-C while models also adjust for BMI and age. HF diagnosed from hospital records, so outpatient cases may be missed.
