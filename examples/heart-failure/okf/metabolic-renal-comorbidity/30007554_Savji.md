# Bottom line

Obesity and insulin resistance are more strongly associated with future HFpEF than HFrEF, especially obesity in women. HOMA-IR was estimated to mediate about 26-29% of the BMI-HFpEF association.

- **Design** — Pooled analysis of four prospective community-based cohorts (CHS, FHS, MESA, PREVEND) using cause-specific Cox models
- **N** — 22,681
- **Population** — Community-dwelling adults aged 30+ without prevalent HF from four US/Dutch cohorts
- **Outcome** — Adjudicated incident HF within 15 years, classified as HFpEF (LVEF>50%) or HFrEF (LVEF<=50%), mostly by echocardiography; unclassified HF treated as competing event
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.86)

# Caveats

Observational pooled cohorts with heterogeneity between cohorts; 30% of HF events unclassified and participants with missing covariates excluded. Mediation by HOMA-IR was exploratory; HFpEF/HFrEF cutoff of EF 50% and EF assessed at time of HF only.
