# Bottom line

Insulin resistance by the conventional HOMA-IR >=2.5 threshold was not significantly associated with incident HF after adjustment, but spline analysis showed risk increasing from HOMA-IR ~1.0 to ~2.25 and then flattening. Obesity and race did not modify the association, and interim MI did not mediate it.

- **Design** — Prospective community-based cohort (ARIC), median 20.6 years follow-up, Cox models with restricted cubic splines
- **N** — 12,606
- **Population** — Middle-aged (45-64 y) ARIC participants without diabetes, prevalent HF or prior MI at baseline
- **Outcome** — First HF hospitalization with ICD-9 428.x in any position or death certificate with HF, through 2009
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.84)

# Caveats

Observational; HF ascertained from ICD-9 codes and death certificates without HF type. Single baseline HOMA-IR measurement; residual confounding possible. Threshold-specific findings are exploratory and the authors did not intend to set a new cutoff.
