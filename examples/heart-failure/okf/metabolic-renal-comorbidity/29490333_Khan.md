# Bottom line

Overweight, obesity and morbid obesity were associated with progressively higher competing hazards of incident CVD, and BMI had the strongest association with heart failure among CVD subtypes. Overweight and obese adults lived more years with CVD.

- **Design** — Pooled individual-level analysis of 10 large US prospective cohorts with modified Kaplan-Meier lifetime risk, competing-risk Cox models and Irwin restricted mean
- **Population** — US adults free of CVD at baseline (ages 20-79) from 10 prospective cohorts, 73.9% female
- **Outcome** — Total CVD and subtypes: fatal and nonfatal coronary heart disease, stroke, congestive heart failure, other CVD deaths
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.71) — nothing to score on size
