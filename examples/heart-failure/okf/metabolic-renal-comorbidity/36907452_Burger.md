# Bottom line

In non-diabetic patients with established CVD, metabolic syndrome and insulin resistance increased incident HF risk independent of established risk factors. Of the MetS components only waist circumference was independently associated.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort (UCC-SMART) with Cox proportional hazards models
- **N** — 4,653
- **Population** — Non-diabetic adults with established cardiovascular disease, without HF at baseline
- **Outcome** — First hospitalization for heart failure
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.73)
