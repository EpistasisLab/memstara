# Bottom line

Higher residential green space and natural environment coverage were associated with lower incident HF risk (HR 0.85-0.88 for the highest tertile). PM2.5 and physical activity partially mediated these associations.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort study using UK Biobank data, Cox models and causal mediation analysis
- **Population** — UK Biobank adult participants
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure over median 13.5 years follow-up (16,209 cases)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.71) — nothing to score on size
