# Bottom line

Historical redlining (Q4 vs Q1-3) was associated with higher incident HF hospitalization among Black beneficiaries (RR 1.08; 1.04 after SDI adjustment) and slightly lower risk among White beneficiaries. SDI explained about 50% of the association and modified it.

- **Design** — Retrospective cohort of Medicare fee-for-service beneficiaries linked to Zip-code level redlining (HOLC) and Social Deprivation Index data, 2014-2019
- **N** — 2,388,955
- **Population** — Non-Hispanic Black and White Medicare fee-for-service beneficiaries aged 40+ without prior HF or MI, in 1044 Zip-codes
- **Outcome** — Incident HF: hospitalization with primary diagnosis of HF (ICD-9 428.*, 402.x, 404.x; ICD-10 I50.*, I11.0, I13.0, I13.2) through Dec 2019
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.86)

Quoted from the paper, by row:

1. This association persisted after adjusting for comorbidities (RR[95%CI]: 1.08[1.04–1.12] P<0.001, Model-2 Table-2).
2. Among Black beneficiaries, the relationship between living in redlined Zip-codes and HF risk attenuated in magnitude but remained statistically significant after adjustment for SDI (RR[95% CI]: 1.04[1.002–1.08], P=0.04, Model-3 Table-2].
3. In adjusted models, living in Zip-codes with high redlining proportion was associated with a modestly lower risk of HF after adjustment for age, sex, and comorbid diseases (Q4 vs. Q3-Q1, RR[95% CI]: 0.94[0.89–0.99], P=0.02, Model-2 Table-2).
4. White participants living in redlined Zip-codes had a modestly lower risk of HF after accounting for SDI [RR[95% CI]: 0.90[0.85–0.95], P<0.001, Model-3 Table-2].
5. Similar patterns of association were observed using a continuous measure of redlining proportion with a higher risk of HF among beneficiaries living in areas with a greater proportion of redlining (RR[95% CI]: 1.04[1.03–1.06], P<0.001) (Figure-1, Table S1).
6. Consistent with primary analysis, among Black adults, higher HOLC score was significantly associated with a higher risk of HF after adjustment for age, sex, and comorbidities (Q4 vs. Q1-3, RR[95%CI]: 1.08[1.04–1.12], P<0.001, Model-2 Table S2].

# Caveats

Outcome is HF hospitalization with primary diagnosis in fee-for-service Medicare only, excluding Medicare Advantage and other races. Zip-code level exposure with exclusion of many Zip-codes (high HOLC missingness) limits generalizability; residual confounding likely and the inverse association in White beneficiaries is unexplained (hip fracture also inverse).
