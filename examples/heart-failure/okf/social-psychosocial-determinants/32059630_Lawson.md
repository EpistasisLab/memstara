# Bottom line

Among incident HF patients, the most deprived were diagnosed about 5 years younger than the most affluent and had worse comorbidity, BMI and smoking trends. South Asian and black patients presented 6 and 9 years younger than whites, with more diabetes and hypertension.

- **Design** — Population-based study using UK general practice database linked to hospitalizations (1998-2017), logistic and linear regression of risk factor trends in incident HF patients
- **Population** — Adults with incident heart failure in UK general practice records linked to hospital data, 1998-2017
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure identified from linked primary care and hospitalization records; analysis compares risk factor profiles at diagnosis
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.65) — nothing to score on size
