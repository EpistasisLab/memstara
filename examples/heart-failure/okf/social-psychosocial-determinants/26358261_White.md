# Bottom line

Major depressive disorder was an independent risk factor for incident heart failure in both HIV-negative and HIV-positive veterans, with the highest risk in co-occurring HIV and MDD. Baseline antidepressant use among those with MDD was associated with lower HF risk.

- **Design** — Prospective longitudinal cohort (Veterans Aging Cohort Study) using electronic medical record ICD-9 data, Cox proportional hazards models
- **N** — 81,427
- **Population** — US veterans free of cardiovascular disease at baseline, HIV+ and matched HIV- (VACS)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure from VA and Medicare ICD-9 codes (428.xx, 429.3, 402.11, 402.91, 425.x); follow-up to HF, death or 12/31/2009
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.89)

# Caveats

Depression and HF were defined from administrative ICD-9 codes, so misclassification and detection bias are possible. Antidepressant analysis had few non-users (about 10% of MDD) and may reflect confounding by indication; depression severity and psychosocial factors were not measured. Veteran population is overwhelmingly male.
