# Bottom line

Hypertension and diabetes were the strongest predictors of incident HF (HR 1.69 and 1.70), with hypertension giving the largest PAR (31.8%). Black participants showed only these two factors; smoking, high BMI and (in women) inactivity also predicted HF in whites.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort (Southern Community Cohort Study) with linkage to CMS claims
- **N** — 27,078
- **Population** — Low-income black and white adults aged 40-79 in the southeastern US, enrolled 2002-2009, receiving Medicare/Medicaid, without prevalent HF
- **Outcome** — First CMS claim with ICD-9 428.x from enrollment to 31 Dec 2010; censored at HF, death or end of 2010
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.86)

# Caveats

Risk factors were self-reported at a single baseline and missing values were coded as absent. HF was identified from claims codes only, in a selected low-income Medicare/Medicaid subset with short follow-up. Socioeconomic factors were covariates, not studied as predictors.
