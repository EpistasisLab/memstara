# Bottom line

Depression roughly doubled to tripled the risk of incident heart failure (adjusted HR 2.59; 2.82 after adjusting for incident MI) in older persons with isolated systolic hypertension. The association did not appear to be mediated by myocardial infarction.

- **Design** — Secondary analysis of a randomized trial cohort (SHEP) with Cox proportional hazards regression
- **N** — 4,538
- **Population** — Adults aged 60+ with isolated systolic hypertension enrolled in SHEP
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during average 4.5 years follow-up
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.73)
