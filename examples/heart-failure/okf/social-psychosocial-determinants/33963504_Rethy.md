# Bottom line

High neighborhood poverty was independently associated with incident HF (adjusted OR 1.80, 1.35-2.39) versus low-poverty neighborhoods. Findings support neighborhood-level interventions alongside individual-level strategies.

- **Design** — Retrospective cohort study of electronic health records, generalized linear mixed effect models
- **N** — 28,858
- **Population** — Northwestern Medicine patients aged 30-80, free of cardiovascular disease at index visit (2005-2013), followed at least 5 years
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure diagnosis in the electronic health record (522 cases, 1.8%)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.71)
