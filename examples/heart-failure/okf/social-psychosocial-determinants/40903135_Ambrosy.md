# Bottom line

Most AANHPI subgroups had lower adjusted incident HF risk than non-Hispanic White adults, but Native Hawaiian/Other Pacific Islander and South Asian adults had higher risk. Disaggregation is needed to see these differences.

- **Design** — Retrospective cohort using Kaiser Permanente Northern California and Hawaii electronic health records, 2012-2022, follow-up through 2023
- **N** — 2,652,271
- **Population** — Adults aged >=30 years without cardiovascular disease in Kaiser Permanente Northern California and Hawaii
- **Outcome** — Incident HF: hospitalization, >=3 clinic visits, or death attributed to HF based on validated diagnostic codes from EHR and mortality data
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.77)
