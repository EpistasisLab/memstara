# Bottom line

Among hypertensive patients, greater joint control of cardiovascular risk factors was associated with stepwise lower incident HF, with ≥6 controlled giving 62% lower risk. At ≥6 controlled factors, HF risk was lower than in nonhypertensive controls.

- **Design** — Prospective cohort (UK Biobank) with matched nonhypertensive controls, Cox proportional hazards models
- **Population** — UK Biobank adults with diagnosed hypertension (75,293) and matched nonhypertensive controls (256,619)
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure during follow-up until May 31, 2021 (abstract does not give coding details)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.59) — nothing to score on size or adjustment

# Caveats

Only the abstract was read, so covariate adjustment and outcome ascertainment are not known. Observational UK Biobank data (healthy volunteer bias); control status is probably measured once at baseline. Effects were stronger in men and medication users.
