# Bottom line

Preeclampsia independently raised future heart failure risk about 4-fold (RR 4.19) in adjusted pooled studies. The association persisted after adjustment for age, BMI and diabetes.

- **Design** — Systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis of MEDLINE and EMBASE studies
- **Population** — Women with and without a history of preeclampsia (>258,000 with preeclampsia) across 22 studies
- **Outcome** — Future incident heart failure (also CHD, CVD death, stroke), as defined in the included studies
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.79) — nothing to score on size

# Caveats

Based on abstract only; heterogeneity, follow-up duration and number of heart failure studies are not shown. Pooled observational data may have residual confounding. Preeclampsia is a pregnancy-specific exposure, limiting generalizability.
