# Bottom line

About 1 in 8 post-PCI CAD patients developed new HF over 63 months, most often HFpEF. Higher BNP, E/e' and AF and lower eGFR raised risk, ACEI/ARB use lowered it, and hypertension and prior MI were not independent predictors.

- **Design** — Prospective longitudinal single-centre cohort with Fine-Gray competing-risk regression (death as competing risk)
- **N** — 3,910
- **Population** — Adults with symptomatic CAD and LVEF ≥50%, no prior HF, undergoing PCI at one Shanghai hospital 2014-2018
- **Outcome** — New-onset HF diagnosed per 2016 ESC guideline during follow-up every 1-3 months; subtyped by LVEF (HFrEF <40, HFmrEF 40-49, HFpEF ≥50%)
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.81)

# Caveats

Single-centre Chinese cohort; HF diagnosis made partly by clinical follow-up, and medication use observed (confounding by indication possible). Medications and echo measured at baseline only; coefficients per unit unclear for continuous variables, and BNP appears possibly log-transformed. Patients with LVEF <50% were excluded.
