# Bottom line

Hypertension, higher systolic and higher diastolic blood pressure were each positively associated with incident heart failure, with a steeper increase at higher levels. RR was 3-5 fold at about 180/120 versus 100/60 mmHg.

- **Design** — Systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of cohort studies (PubMed, Embase to June 2022), random effects models
- **Population** — Adults in 47 cohort studies; general and mixed populations
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure as defined in the included cohort studies (not detailed in abstract)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.66) — nothing to score on size or adjustment
