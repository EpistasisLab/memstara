# Bottom line

Hypertensive patients are at increased risk of congestive heart failure, largely independent of LVH or dysfunction, and BP reduction and control lower that risk. Meta-analyses favour diuretics and renin-angiotensin blockers over calcium antagonists and beta-blockers for preventing new-onset HF.
