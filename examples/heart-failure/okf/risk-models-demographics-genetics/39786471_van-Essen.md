# Bottom line

Lifetime HF risk was similar by sex (24.5% men, 23.3% women), but HFrEF was commoner in men and HFpEF in women. Eight modifiable risk factors accounted for 60-71% of HFrEF and 46-64% of HFpEF, with hypertension the largest contributor.

- **Design** — Community-based prospective cohort (PREVEND) with 25-year follow-up, competing-risk lifetime risk and PAF analysis
- **N** — 8,558
- **Population** — Adults aged 28-75 from Groningen, Netherlands, enrolled 1997-98 and followed to 2022
- **Outcome** — Hospital-record adjudicated new-onset HF per 2021 ESC guidelines; HFrEF LVEF<50%, HFpEF LVEF>=50%
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.79)

# Caveats

Risk factors measured once at baseline (1997-98) and treated as fixed over 25 years; ascertainment after 2011 relied on record review. Single Dutch, mostly Caucasian cohort; PAFs assume causality and have wide CIs, and sex-difference tests were borderline (P=.062, .049). No risk score or genetic data.
