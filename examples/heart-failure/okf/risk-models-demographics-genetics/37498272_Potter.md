# Bottom line

Clinical ARIC risk separates low and high HF risk, while echocardiography (GLS, diastolic function, LVH) adds incremental prediction mainly in the intermediate group. Abnormal GLS was the strongest independent predictor (HR 2.92).

- **Design** — Training cohort (n=926, 7-year follow-up) and validation cohort (n=355, 10-year follow-up) with CART-derived risk stratification algorithm
- **Population** — Adults with HF risk factors (stage A or stage B HF) without prior HF, training and validation groups
- **Outcome** — Incident heart failure (including new HF admission) during follow-up
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.55) — nothing to score on size
