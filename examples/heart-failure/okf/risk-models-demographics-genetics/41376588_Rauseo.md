# Bottom line

Three hypertension clusters stratified incident heart failure and other events, with cluster 2 (male, high atherosclerosis burden) at highest risk (HF HR 1.77), largely mediated by LV hypertrophy. Cluster membership added only modest prognostic information (Nagelkerke R2 <0.02).

- **Design** — Retrospective analysis of prospectively collected UK Biobank cohort; unsupervised clustering with Cox and mediation analyses
- **Population** — UK Biobank adults with diagnosed hypertension and CMR imaging, free of prevalent heart failure
- **Outcome** — Incident all-cause HF after imaging visit (HF, pulmonary edema, or any cardiomyopathy) from UK Biobank linked records, censored at death or 31 Oct 2022
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.81) — nothing to score on size

# Caveats

Predominantly White UK Biobank volunteers with CMR imaging (healthy-volunteer selection); clusters are sex-dominated (cluster 2 99.9% male). Few HF events/low explanatory power (pseudo-R2 <0.02); no external validation; HF HRs for cluster 3 not reported in text.
