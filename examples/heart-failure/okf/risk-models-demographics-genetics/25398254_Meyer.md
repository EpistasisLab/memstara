# Bottom line

Men developed heart failure more often and earlier, but women had higher adjusted risk of HFpEF (SHR 2.16) and lower risk of HFrEF (SHR 0.47). Atrial fibrillation was a female-specific risk factor (P for interaction 0.016).

- **Design** — Prospective population-based cohort (PREVEND) with competing risks analysis
- **N** — 8,592
- **Population** — Middle-aged community-dwelling adults (mean age 49.2 years; 50.1% women) in the Netherlands PREVEND study
- **Outcome** — New-onset heart failure, subtyped as HFrEF (EF <40%) or HFpEF (EF >50%)
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.74)
