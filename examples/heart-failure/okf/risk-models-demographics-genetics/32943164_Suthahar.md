# Bottom line

Clinical risk factors were strongly and similarly associated with incident HF in both sexes (C-statistic 0.80 men, 0.83 women), with only age showing significant sex interaction. Adding natriuretic peptides, troponins or CRP improved discrimination only marginally.

- **Design** — Pooled analysis of 4 prospective community-based cohorts (FHS, PREVEND, MESA, CHS) with Fine-Gray competing risk models
- **N** — 22,756
- **Population** — Community-dwelling adults aged 30+ free of heart failure at baseline, 53% women
- **Outcome** — Adjudicated incident HF by signs and symptoms from medical record review; subtyped HFpEF (LVEF>=50%) vs HFrEF; follow-up truncated at 15 years, death as competing risk
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.86)

# Caveats

Observational pooled cohorts with harmonized definitions; 1,570 excluded for missing covariates and biomarkers varied by cohort. Single baseline biomarker measurement; incremental value assessed in subset with all biomarkers and C-statistic gains not externally validated.
