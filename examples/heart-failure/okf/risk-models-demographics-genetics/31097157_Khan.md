# Bottom line

Race- and sex-specific Cox equations using age, BP, glucose, BMI, cholesterol, smoking and QRS duration predicted 10-year HF with good discrimination (C 0.71-0.88) and calibration across internal and external cohorts. Non-HF CVD scores discriminated similarly but were poorly calibrated.

- **Design** — Pooled individual-level analysis of 5 community cohorts with random split derivation/internal validation, plus external validation in 2 cohorts
- **N** — 33,010
- **Population** — Black and white adults aged 30-80 free of CVD from community-based US and European cohorts
- **Outcome** — Adjudicated incident heart failure within 10 years (censored at 12 years; non-HF death censored)
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.75)

# Caveats

Only black and white adults; HF ascertainment differed across cohorts. Predictors exclude biomarkers (e.g., natriuretic peptide), and no HF-specific prior scores were compared. Coefficient-level effects were not reported with CIs.
