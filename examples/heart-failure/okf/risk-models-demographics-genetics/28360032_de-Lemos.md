# Bottom line

A five-test multimodality panel improved discrimination and reclassification for global CVD and incident heart failure beyond traditional risk factors in MESA and DHS. A simple count of abnormal tests gave graded risk (HR 7.5 for score ≥4).

- **Design** — Two prospective population-based cohorts (MESA, DHS) with >10 years follow-up; Cox models, c-statistic, NRI, IDI
- **Population** — Adults free of known CVD in MESA (n=6621, age 45-84) and Dallas Heart Study (n=2202, age 30-65)
- **Outcome** — Primary: global CVD composite (CV death, MI, stroke, revascularization, incident HF, AF); heart failure was a tertiary endpoint, adjudicated by blinded committees
- **Read from** — the full text
- **Evidence strength** — strong (0.81) — nothing to score on size

# Caveats

Heart failure results were mostly in MESA only; DHS had just 28 HF events, so only univariable analyses there. Same-cohort model derivation with no external validation of coefficients, and cutpoints were applied to complete-case participants. Heart failure was part of a composite outcome and not a primary focus.
