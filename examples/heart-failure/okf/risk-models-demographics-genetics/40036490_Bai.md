# Bottom line

XGBoost using seven simple composite indices reported an AUC of 0.96 for prevalent heart failure in elderly adults with diabetes and hypertension, with BRI and PNI most influential. The outcome is HF status at survey, not incident HF, so the result is not prospective prediction.

- **Design** — Cross-sectional analysis of NHANES 2003-2016 with nine machine learning models and SHAP feature importance
- **N** — 1,445
- **Population** — US adults aged 65+ with both diabetes and hypertension (NHANES 2003-2016)
- **Outcome** — Heart failure status (self-reported/survey-recorded HF) at time of survey
- **Read from** — the abstract only

Cross-sectional, so predictors and HF are measured together and reverse causation is likely. AUC of 0.96 in a small sample with no external validation suggests overfitting; abstract gives no per-feature effects or calibration. Narrow population limits generalizability.
