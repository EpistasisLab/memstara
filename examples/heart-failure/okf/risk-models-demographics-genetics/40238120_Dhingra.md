# Bottom line

A single-lead AI-ECG LVSD probability predicted incident HF hospitalization across three cohorts, independent of age, sex, comorbidities and competing death risk, and improved discrimination over PCP-HF and PREVENT. UKB and ELSA-Brasil had very few events (46 and 31).

- **Design** — Retrospective cohort in YNHHS plus prospective population cohorts (UK Biobank, ELSA-Brasil)
- **Population** — Adults without baseline HF with outpatient ECGs: YNHHS (192,667), UK Biobank (42,141), ELSA-Brasil (13,454)
- **Outcome** — New-onset HF, defined as first HF hospitalization
- **Read from** — the abstract only
- **Evidence strength** — moderate (0.65) — nothing to score on size

Abstract only. Very few HF events in UKB (46) and ELSA-Brasil (31) give wide CIs. HF defined as first hospitalization; ECGs in YNHHS were clinically indicated, not wearable recordings, and prospective validation is lacking.
