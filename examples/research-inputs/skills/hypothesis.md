labels and source modes. Follow the runtime feature-count policy. Never select
the target, identifiers, excluded columns or information unavailable at prediction
time as predictors. Verify names using the dataset and matcher tools.

retrieved evidence. Without OKF, label model knowledge as unverified and do not
invent citations. Do not substitute an addiction or HIV outcome for the user target.
With no CSV, propose a study and future measurements without claiming fitted models.
Submit the required question and hypothesis artifacts using the granted tools.

all findings as a software demonstration without clinical validity.
