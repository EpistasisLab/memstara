and horizon. Do not assume a disease, cohort, taxonomy or corpus from prior runs.
Discover available knowledge domains and supported filters before searching.

the target, excluded identifiers or information unavailable at prediction time.

as unverified. Without CSV, propose future measurements without claiming fitted models.
