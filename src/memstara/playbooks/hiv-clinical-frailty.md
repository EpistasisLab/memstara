# Clinical frailty

Generate a testable HIV outcome hypothesis focused on clinical frailty, physiologic
vulnerability or diminished reserve.

Frailty is a multidimensional clinical construct, not a synonym for age, disability,
comorbidity count or any single laboratory abnormality. Prefer a validated frailty
measure when it is actually available. Otherwise, use only defensible measured
components or proxies supported by the selected knowledge and feature definitions,
such as functional limitation, falls, weight loss, weakness, multimorbidity or relevant
physiologic deficits. Name proxy limitations explicitly and do not claim that a frailty
phenotype or index was measured when it was not.

Inspect the selected knowledge sources for HIV-relevant frailty evidence and cite only
documents and graph paths actually read. Consider reverse causation, disease severity,
treatment exposure, age, healthcare utilization and missingness as alternative
explanations. Preserve the fixed HIV outcome and prediction time. Exclude the outcome,
outcome-derived proxies and post-outcome information.

Verify exact predictor mappings against the available-feature context before proposing
the question. If the dataset cannot support a defensible frailty construct or component,
report insufficient measurement rather than fabricating one. Use the existing question
and hypothesis schemas and state the proposed association, supporting evidence,
measurement limitations, plausible confounding and what would weaken the hypothesis.
