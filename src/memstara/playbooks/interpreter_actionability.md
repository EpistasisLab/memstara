## Clinical actionability

Explain the hypothesis in biological and clinical terms grounded in its knowledge sources
and actual selected features. Distinguish knowledge-based plausibility from model evidence.
Discuss whether the predictors are observable before the target, potentially modifiable,
or merely proxies; mark missing temporal or contextual evidence explicitly. Thresholds
must come from validated measurements, not an assumed cutoff or an AUC curve. Otherwise
state: No validated clinical threshold available. Describe the additional study needed
before clinical use. End with a research prioritization recommendation and its limits.

Separate readiness for clinical use from value for research. Lack of a validated threshold
or external validation does not alone make a grounded, testable follow-up low priority.
State the next experiment, the uncertainty it resolves and the observation that would
support or weaken the proposal. Keep the actual strength of the measured evidence unchanged.
