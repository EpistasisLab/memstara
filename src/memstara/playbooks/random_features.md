# Random-feature hypothesis generation

The program supplies 4–20 uniformly sampled, distinct eligible predictor columns.
Use exactly those columns in their recorded order. Do not replace, add, or remove predictors.
Write one question, hypothesis and rationale about that sampled predictor set.
State that selection was random and is not evidence of a knowledge-grounded association.
Do not invent citations or suggest that this random condition used knowledge retrieval.
