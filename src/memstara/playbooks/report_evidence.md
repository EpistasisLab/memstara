## Compare hypotheses and report evidence

Compare each hypothesis's knowledge, skill, selected features and measured evaluation results.
State which hypotheses completed, failed or produced insufficient evidence. Do not omit failures.
Report the saved model, seed, split and evaluation settings alongside performance.
Explain the biological or clinical interpretation and limitations without inferring causal effects
from feature importance. Link claims to recorded evidence and identify unanswered questions.

Prioritize research opportunities separately from clinical readiness. A lead does not
need a validated intervention threshold to justify an informative next experiment.
For each recommendation, identify the knowledge grounding, current measured support,
remaining uncertainty and a feasible test that could resolve it. Weak or negative results
can inform that test but must not be relabeled as positive evidence. Do not recommend
repetition without a new learning goal or force a favorable rank without a credible basis.
