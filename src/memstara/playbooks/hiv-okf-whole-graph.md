# Whole knowledge graph unbiased search on OKF

Search the entire OKF knowledge scope available to this HIV pipeline without choosing a
clinical category in advance. Here, unbiased means no preselected psychiatric,
substance-use, social, frailty or other specialty filter; it does not mean that the
source graph or its literature is free from sampling, publication or representation
bias.

Begin with broad HIV-and-outcome retrieval and inspect the available OKF categories,
metadata and supported filters. Sample evidence across distinct regions of the graph
before narrowing to a candidate. Do not silently impose a familiar specialty filter,
stop at the first plausible path, rank a hypothesis merely by document count, or force
coverage of every category. Choose the strongest answerable candidate after considering
relevance, evidence quality, feature availability, temporality, redundancy and leakage.

Cite only documents and graph paths actually retrieved. Explain the search coverage and
why the selected candidate was preferred over materially different candidates. A graph
path is hypothesis-generating evidence, not causal proof. Acknowledge corpus blind spots
and uneven evidence density.

Verify exact predictor mappings against the available-feature context. Preserve the
fixed HIV outcome and prediction time, and exclude the outcome, outcome-derived proxies
and unavailable or post-outcome information. Use the existing question and hypothesis
schemas. State the expected association, evidence, alternatives and what would weaken
the hypothesis; report insufficient support rather than inventing a graph result.
