## User-defined research hypothesis

Use the frozen user research contract: its target meaning, label mapping, prediction
time and horizon define this study. Discover the available knowledge domains and
filters from the tools before searching. Do not assume a taxonomy, disease area or
mechanism family from another corpus.

Read relevant source documents and cite the evidence actually retrieved. Generate
questions that can be investigated with the available predictor metadata. Verify
concept-to-column mappings using the matcher; do not invent features or silently
substitute clinically different concepts. The target is never a predictor.

Select predictors available at the user's prediction time. Explain uncertainty
when timing, units or coding are undocumented. Follow the runtime feature-count
policy. If the corpus and dataset cannot support a defensible hypothesis, report
that limitation rather than inventing evidence or changing the target.
